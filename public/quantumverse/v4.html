Ask the team ↗
What is Quantumverse?＋

Quantumverse is a quantum computing event by Quantum Feynatics at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus. The previous edition combined workshops, expert sessions, and a hackathon. Vol. 4.0 is the next chapter.

Do I need a background in quantum computing?＋

The proposed programme starts with foundations. Curiosity and some familiarity with Python will help you follow the practical sessions. Final prerequisites and eligibility will be published with registration.

When and where is Vol. 4.0 happening?＋

The community is based at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus. Vol. 4.0 dates, venue arrangements, and attendance format have not yet been announced.

How do I register? Is there a fee?＋

Registration is not open yet. Fees, eligibility, and the official registration link will be published here once confirmed. You can email the organizers to express interest.

What should I bring?＋

Plan for a laptop, charger, and an eagerness to experiment. Software setup instructions and any additional requirements will be shared with the final programme.

Can I sponsor, speak, or volunteer?＋

Get in touch at ieee.sb@hyderabad.bits-pilani.ac.in with your interests. The team can discuss opportunities and the plans for Vol. 4.0.
